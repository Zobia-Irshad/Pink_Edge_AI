PINK EDGE AI — BILL OF MATERIALS (BOM)
========================================
Recommended pilot build: RK3588 SBC + ESP32 GSM companion (see ALTERNATIVES.md, Option A).
Quantities below are per single edge node. Prices are rough 2026 USD street estimates for
planning only — get real supplier quotes before ordering.

MAIN COMPUTE
------------
1x  RK3588(S) SBC, 4-8GB RAM (Orange Pi 5 / Radxa Rock 5B or equivalent)     $60-120
1x  microSD card, 64-128GB, A2/UHS-I rated                                   $10-20
1x  Heatsink + 5V cooling fan (board-matched)                                $5-10
1x  USB UVC camera OR CSI camera module (image capture)                      $10-40
1x  5V/4A USB-C PD or barrel-jack power supply                               $8-15

GSM / TELEMETRY
---------------
1x  ESP32 DevKitC (companion MCU for GSM handling)                           $4-8
1x  SIM800L GSM module (with onboard regulator breakout preferred)           $5-10
1x  2G GSM whip antenna, u.FL or SMA connector                               $2-5
1x  Local carrier SIM card (2G-capable, data or SMS plan)                    ongoing cost
2x  Status LED (5mm) + 220-ohm resistor                                      <$1
1x  18650 Li-ion cell + TP4056 charge module (optional battery backup)       $5-10

ENCLOSURE / POWER RESILIENCE
-----------------------------
1x  IP54+ ventilated enclosure (or custom/3D-printed case)                   $10-30
1x  UPS HAT or 12V/7Ah SLA battery + charge controller (optional)            $15-40
1x  7-10" HDMI/DSI touchscreen (optional, for local UI without a laptop)     $30-60

CABLING / MISC
--------------
1x  Dupont jumper wire kit (M-M, M-F, F-F)                                   $3-5
1x  Breadboard or perfboard (prototyping before a permanent enclosure)       $3-8
1x  USB-A to Micro/Type-C cable (camera/accessory connections as needed)     $2-5

ESTIMATED TOTAL PER NODE
-------------------------
Minimum (no display, no battery backup):    ~$110-190
With display:                               ~$140-250
With display + battery backup:              ~$170-300
